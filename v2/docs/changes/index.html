Psi4 e95c2d234, 2026-10-19

  • A two-orbital pair keeps its orbitals in their slots when its FON crosses 1, so the printed n_a can be below 1; only generation-0 geminals wider than two are reordered to descending occupation (§12).
  • Performance optimization of SI: catalog reading, FON relaxation, overlap blocks, Davidson multiply; memory optimization of SI.

Psi4 72ce4d58b, 2026-10-17

  • Property headers name their switches in brackets, e.g. [DIPOLE], [SUMMARY]; several blocks are renamed, natural orbitals print before the transitions, State analysis is alphabetical (Print order, SI_REKS_ANALYSIS); the CT columns are CT, PR_h, PR_e, PR (Fragment charge-transfer descriptors).
  • State summary lists the three largest weights ≥ 1 % per state, then every further one > 20 %; the overlap pairs under Adiabatic states print from level 3 (SSR state energies, REKS_REPORT_LEVEL).
  • Davidson: search space min(r, max(40, 16k)), start vectors and preconditioner from the P space, Jacobi–Davidson corrections; with the default counts the shipped catalogs solve the whole space (SI eigensolver, SI Davidson solver).

Psi4 bd0fb43ef, 2026-10-15

  • Each SI cassette is solved by LAPACK (every root) or by block Davidson (the lowest roots), SI_REKS_SOLVER; on a Davidson cassette a count -1 means the 50 lowest roots (SI eigensolver, SI Davidson solver).
  • SI_REKS_GRAD, SI_REKS_NAC, SI_REKS_PROPERTY_FOR and SI_REKS_STORE_STATES take one entry per SI cassette in the spin order of SI_REKS_2SPIN (spin groups as listed there, cassettes of a group in their SI_REKS_CONFIGS order); the printed K stays in ascending 2S (Cassette order).
  • The SI-only FONs stay in [m, 2 − m], m set by the SI FON bound margin (SI-only FONs, FON micro-solver).
  • Added: SI_REKS_DAVIDSON_R_CONVERGENCE
  • Added: SI_REKS_FON_BOUND_MARGIN
  • Added: SI_REKS_SOLVER

Psi4 d40267fc2, 2026-10-13

  • One-electron SI couplings are Slater–Condon elements over the Fock matrices of the microstates, the bra/ket mean for an excitation (SI Hamiltonian and overlap); SSR energies and state properties follow from them.
  • SI gradients and NAC are analytic with meta-GGA functionals (Nuclear gradients of SSR states, Nonadiabatic coupling vectors); Laplacian-dependent meta-GGAs are rejected with an input error.
  • NTO holes, NDOs, state natural orbitals and EKT eigenvectors are in a canonical basis of each degenerate span (Natural transition orbitals, Natural orbitals and occupations of the active space).
