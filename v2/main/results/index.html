Results

Ensemble DFT (REKS, SSR states) for broken bonds, diradicals and excited states, checked four ways.

REKS: ensemble Kohn–Sham orbitals with fractional occupations. SSR (SI-SA-REKS): the adiabatic states built on these orbitals.

Komarov et al., ChemRxiv 2026

(6,6) multireference excited states, 1260 basis functions, in less wall time than ORCA TDA and SF-TDA.
