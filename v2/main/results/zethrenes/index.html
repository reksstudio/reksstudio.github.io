Zethrenes and related PAHs, REKS(2,2) to (10,10)

Seven PAHs of Hu et al. (2016), 32–70 atoms, 20 geometries (three per compound, two for IF); SA-REKS and SI-SA-REKS from (2,2) to (10,10), BH&HLYP/cc-pVTZ; 98 calculations; energies, occupations and active orbitals of every one, with its input.

Reference class: trend without reference

Definitions

ΔEST = E(T1) − E(S0), ΔESQ = E(Q1) − E(S0)

Same geometry, same active space; S0, T1, Q1 = lowest state with 2S = 0, 2, 4; positive: T1 (Q1) above S0. Spin gaps and ΔEgeom in kcal mol⁻¹ (1 decimal, with sign); SI state energies E − E(S0) in eV.

Rcs, Ros, RT: optima of the closed-shell singlet, the broken-symmetry open-shell singlet and the triplet (SI appendix blocks “Singlet Closed Shell”, “Singlet Biradical Open Shell”, “Triplet”; UCAM-B3LYP/6-31G*, Hu et al. 2016, SI p. S16).

SA-REKS: ensemble SCF, gives the orbitals and their FONs · SI: configurations of one spin manifold diagonalized in the SA-REKS orbitals, gives the states S0, T1, Q1, … · FON: fractional occupation of an active orbital in the SA-REKS ensemble · natural occupation: eigenvalue of the active 1-RDM of one SI state.

Setup
