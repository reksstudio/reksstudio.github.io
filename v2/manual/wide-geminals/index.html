4. Wide Geminals

A geminal of width w holds two electrons in w orbitals (REKS_GEMINAL_WIDTHS). Example: BH. The boron lone pair correlates with both π orbitals, REKS(2,3); the B–H bond pair is added in REKS(4,5) and REKS(4,6). Procedure as in §3: seed → slots → start → check. The orbital part of this page runs HF with reks_fon_interp_delta 1010: then f(x) = √x (Coupling Δ and factor f) and E(PPS1) is the energy of a product of geminals, which is checked exactly (Check the solution).

Geminal of width w

The PPS geminal over the slots p1, …, pw of one geminal:

Φ0=∑k=1wsknpk2|pk𝑝̄k|,∑k=1wnpk=2,s=(+,−,…,−).

For w = 2 this is Φ0 of Geminals. The first slot of each geminal carries the + sign; in a seed it holds a doubly occupied orbital. Geminals of scheme 0, as Pairing scheme 0 prints them in Post-Iterations:

reks reks_geminal_widths Geminals of scheme 0
[2, 3] [3] (a,b,c)
[2, 4] [4] (a,b,c,d)
[4, 5] [2, 3] (a,c,d) & (b,e)
[4, 6] [3, 3] (a,d,f) & (b,c,e)

Token k of GUESS_ACTIVE_WINDOW fills slot k (Start REKS); the window lists the orbitals in slot order a, b, c, …, not geminal by geminal.

A file written by REKS stores the core, then the active orbitals in slot order a, b, …, then the virtuals; the kA labels of Post-Iterations number the orbitals by ε and are not columns. Check the solution

Exact energy
with HF and δ = 1010, E(PPS1) equals ⟨Ψ|H|Ψ⟩ of the product Ψ of the geminals Φ0 (Geminal of width w) in the converged orbitals, with np from Post-Iterations; H is the Hamiltonian of the active space with the core frozen. Minimizing ⟨Ψ|H|Ψ⟩ over the coefficients at fixed orbitals lowers nothing:
wide_bh_23_reks23    (a,b,c)            E(PPS1) -25.1640880605  E(product) - E(PPS1) -5.7e-12  min over coefficients -5.8e-12  CASCI -25.1640880605
wide_bh_45_reks44    (a,d) & (b,c)      E(PPS1) -25.1540332485  E(product) - E(PPS1) -4.3e-12  min over coefficients -5.7e-12  CASCI -25.1543465599
wide_bh_45_reks45    (a,c,d) & (b,e)    E(PPS1) -25.1775062082  E(product) - E(PPS1) -9.7e-12  min over coefficients -1.0e-11  CASCI -25.1786807010
wide_bh_45_reks46    (a,d,f) & (b,c,e)  E(PPS1) -25.1828261871  E(product) - E(PPS1) -8.2e-12  min over coefficients -1.3e-11  CASCI -25.1846729240
  • CASCI in the same orbitals is lower for two geminals (0.31 mEh for REKS(4,4), 1.2 for REKS(4,5), 1.8 for REKS(4,6)): a product of geminals has no correlation between the geminals. For one geminal CASCI = E(PPS1).
  • Equal FONs of the two π are the cylindrical symmetry of BH. With one π in the geminal (REKS(2,2), LUMO+2 in the window) the other π stays empty (Lone pair: REKS(2,3)).

Gradients

All roots of the SI cassette in one gradient() (Gradients and couplings against finite differences), from the converged file of each active space: REKS(2,3) the full cassette, REKS(4,5) and REKS(4,6) every configuration of scheme 0:

                  "si_reks_grad": [list(range(25))]})
g, wfn = psi4.gradient("hf", return_wfn=True, restart_file="wide_bh_reks45.wfn.npy")
