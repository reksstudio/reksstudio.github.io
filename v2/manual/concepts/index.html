2. Concepts

Active space, core, virtual

REKS [N, M] places N active electrons in M active orbitals with fractional occupation. The other occupied orbitals form a doubly occupied core shared by every determinant; the orbitals above the active space are virtual.

Ensemble Kohn–Sham reference and FONs

The non-interacting reference density, with fractional occupation numbers (FONs) nq for the orbitals pseudo-degenerate at the Fermi level, is

ρs=∑k∈core2|φk|2+∑q∈activenq|φq|2, 0≤nq≤2,∑qnq=N.

The energy functional follows from integration over the electron–electron coupling strength λ from 0 to 1 at λ-independent FONs. The orbitals and the FONs are optimized variationally (Filatov, WIREs Comput. Mol. Sci. 2015, 5, 146).

Microstate

A microstate L is a Slater determinant of the shared orbitals with fixed integer spin-orbital occupations nq,Lσ. Its energy is an ordinary Kohn–Sham energy with the chosen functional (local, non-local, hybrid or range-separated):

EL=E[ρLα,ρLβ].

Geminals

An active orbital pair (p,q) holds two electrons; its FONs satisfy

np+nq=2.

The pair is in one of four geminals, the triplet with its three components m=0,±1:

Φ0=12(np|p𝑝̄|−nq|q𝑞̄|)perfectly pairedΦ2=12(nq|p𝑝̄|+np|q𝑞̄|)doubly excitedΦ1=12(|p𝑞̄|+|q𝑝̄|)open-shell singletΦT0=12(|p𝑞̄|−|q𝑝̄|)triplet, m=0ΦT+=|pq|,ΦT−=|𝑝̄𝑞̄|triplet, m=±1

Pairing scheme

A pairing scheme partitions the M active orbitals into P=N/2 geminals. With two-orbital geminals (REKS(M,M)) each geminal is a pair joining one orbital of the lower half with one of the upper half:

P=M/2.

With REKS_GEMINAL_WIDTHS geminal i spans wi orbitals, ∑iwi=M; Pairing schemes prints its first two orbitals.

Scheme 0 pairs the active slots (active columns, from 0, labelled a, b, …) from the outside in: (2,2) (a,b); (4,4) (a,d) & (b,c); (6,6) (a,f) & (b,e) & (c,d):

(k,M−1−k),k=0,…,P−1.

REKS(M,M) with two-orbital geminals has P! schemes, numbered from 0: one for (2,2), two for (4,4), six for (6,6) (§5).

Configuration and its energy

A configuration (geminal product state) is the antisymmetrized product of the core and one geminal per pair of one scheme:

Ψ=Aˆ[(core)Φt1(p1,q1)⋯ΦtP(pP,qP)].

One scheme carries 3P singlet configurations, named by type (§5). Its energy is a weighted sum of microstate energies, the coefficients being functions of the FONs (Filatov, Martínez, Kim, Phys. Chem. Chem. Phys. 2016, 18, 21040):

EX=∑LCLX({n})EL,∑LCLX=1.

Coupling Δ and factor f

The energy expressions contain, for each pair (a,b), the coupling Δab in place of the exchange integral Kab, multiplied by f(x) in place of the FON radical x:

Δab=12[E(a𝑏̄)+E(b𝑎̄)−E(ab)−E(𝑎̄𝑏̄)], f(x)=x1−x+δ2(1+δ),x=nanb.

For REKS(2,2), with f=f(nanb) and na≥1, the configuration energies are

EPPS=na2E(a𝑎̄)+nb2E(b𝑏̄)−fΔabEDES=nb2E(a𝑎̄)+na2E(b𝑏̄)+fΔabEOSS=E(a𝑏̄)+E(b𝑎̄)−12[E(ab)+E(𝑎̄𝑏̄)]ET=12[E(ab)+E(𝑎̄𝑏̄)]

EOSS and ET carry no FON.

Towards the closed-shell limit (na→2) the factor f suppresses the Δ term, which avoids counting correlation twice, by the formalism and by the functional. At na=nb=1 it equals 1 (Moreira et al., J. Chem. Theory Comput. 2007, 3, 764). δ is REKS_FON_INTERP_DELTA (default 0.4, §11).

FON set and generation

The generation of a configuration is its number of open pairs: Φ1, and a triplet geminal (ΦT+, ΦT0, ΦT−) in a sector of positive 2S. A FON set holds the FONs of one generation, pairing scheme and run sector; all configurations of that set use the same FONs. Φ0 and Φ2 of one pair use the same FONs; Φ1 and ΦT0 use none. FON-set labels: FON sets.

SA-REKS ensemble and weights

The SCF minimizes the ensemble energy over the orbitals and the FONs read by the SA configurations, at fixed weights wX (ensemble variational principle: Gross, Oliveira, Kohn, Phys. Rev. A 1988, 37, 2805; SA-REKS: Filatov, Top. Curr. Chem. 2016, 368, 97):

ESA=∑XwXEX=∑LCLEL,CL=∑XwXCLX, 0≤wX≤1,∑XwX=1.

The default weights are equal (SA_REKS_WEIGHTS). ESA is a weighted average of configuration energies, not the energy of a state; the variational principle bounds ESA, not an individual EX. Raw determinants enter the ensemble through SA_REKS_EXTRA.

Orbitals, orbital energies, SCF convergence

The orbitals satisfy, with orbital-specific Fock operators Fˆq,

fqFˆqφq=∑pφpεpq,fq=nq/2.

At convergence the Lagrangian εpq is symmetric (generalized Brillouin condition) but not diagonal, so the orbitals are not canonical. The printed orbital energies are the diagonal of the SA-REKS coupling Fock matrix in the MO basis and carry no Koopmans meaning; ionization energies come from EKT (REKS_EKT). The SCF is converged when ESA is stationary and the orbital gradient vanishes (generalized Brillouin condition, §12).

Spin-polarized configurations

In REKS(4,4) the double open-shell singlet (DOSS) and the doubly spin-polarized state (DSPS) both arise from two simultaneous OSS-type one-electron transitions between the active orbitals: DOSS couples two open-shell singlet pairs to an overall singlet, DSPS can be viewed as two triplet excitations coupled to an overall singlet, and the two are orthogonalized (Filatov, Martínez, Kim, J. Chem. Phys. 2017, 147, 064104). Fully open configurations of different schemes overlap (overlap −½ for the two DOSS configurations of REKS(4,4)); the catalog replaces them by a set orthogonalized by sequential Gram–Schmidt across schemes, the spin-polarized configurations (SPS) DOSS_SPS_k, T_OSS_SPS_k, …, in which each member k after the first is orthogonal to the preceding members (Filatov et al., J. Chem. Phys. 2016, 145, 244104). They carry no FON.

Spin manifold and run sector

A manifold is one total spin 2S of the catalog. The sectors of a run are the manifolds addressed by any SA block or SI group, in ascending 2S, sector 0 being the lowest. Every configuration is built in its component of zero MS, so all sectors share one determinant space and one set of orbitals, and the molecule multiplicity stays canonical (1 for even N) (Active space, §8). Determinants of other MS enter the SA ensemble only through SA_REKS_EXTRA.

A 2S = 2 configuration holds one ΦT0. In 2S ≥ 4 (TT_SPS_0, TTT_SPS_0) the nT triplet geminals couple to S=nT through ΦT+, ΦT0, ΦT− with Clebsch–Gordan coefficients, e.g.

TT_SPS_0=16ΦT−ΦT++23ΦT0ΦT0+16ΦT+ΦT−.

SI cassette

A cassette is a set of configurations of one manifold over which the SI Hamiltonian and overlap are built and diagonalized after the SCF, in the converged SA orbitals. It is chosen independently of the SA pool. A run may hold several cassettes, each its own eigenproblem. The label kSI-lSA-REKS(N,M) denotes k SI configurations in the orbitals of an SA ensemble of l configurations (SI_REKS_CONFIGS).

SI-only FONs

FONs that no SA configuration uses (higher generations, or schemes absent from the SA pool) are optimized once after the SCF, with the SA FONs held fixed. Each SI-only geminal belongs to its primary configuration K, the lowest-index configuration of the cassette among those that use it. Its FONs minimize, at the microstate energies EL of the converged SA orbitals,

EK=∑LCLK({n})EL,1≤np≤2−10−8,

for a two-orbital geminal; in a geminal of width w each free FON is ≥10−8 and their sum ≤2−10−8. These bounds are fixed; REKS_<L>_FON does not apply. The geminals of one generation with the same K are solved jointly; Gauss–Seidel sweeps over these groups repeat until no FON changes by more than 10−7. Output: FONs of the SI-only geminals.

SI Hamiltonian and overlap

The diagonal elements of 𝐇 are the configuration energies EX. The off-diagonal elements are sums over microstate energies EL, spin-Fock differences (Fα−Fβ)pq, Lagrangian elements εpq and active two-electron integrals (pq|rs) (table Off-diagonal channel, REKS_REPORT_LEVEL ≥ 3). They follow from the Slater–Condon rules at small λ and integration over λ (Filatov, Martínez, Kim, J. Chem. Phys. 2017, 147, 064104). 𝐒 is diagonal within one scheme and non-diagonal across schemes, so each cassette solves

𝐇𝐜=E𝐒𝐜.

Over (PPS, OSS, DES) the SSR(2,2) Hamiltonian, with the Lagrangian element εab and 𝐒=𝟏, is

𝐇=(EPPS(na−nb)εab0(na−nb)εabEOSS(na+nb)εab0(na+nb)εabEDES).

Eigenvectors of 𝐒 whose eigenvalue s satisfies

s≤csmax⁡

(smax⁡ the largest eigenvalue of 𝐒, c = SI_REKS_OVERLAP_CUTOFF) are null modes, removed before diagonalization; the rank of 𝐒 depends on the FONs. In full singlet cassettes the 18 configurations of REKS(4,4) span 16 dimensions and the 161 of REKS(6,6) span 115 (SI Hamiltonian and overlap).

Coupling damping below 100 % exact exchange

With a functional whose global (short-range) exact-exchange fraction is below 100 %, range-separated functionals included, the off-diagonal elements Hij that the catalog marks as carried only by four-orbital active integrals (pq|rs) are scaled, energies in Eh (Filatov, Martínez, Kim, J. Chem. Phys. 2017, 147, 064104, Eq. B50; parameters from Grimme, Waletzke, J. Chem. Phys. 1999, 111, 5645):

Hij→0.619exp⁡[−3.27(Hii−Hjj)4]Hij.

At 100 % exact exchange no element is scaled. When an element was scaled, the References block (REKS_REPORT_LEVEL ≥ 3) lists Coupling damping (DFT). The exact-exchange fraction is in the XC evaluation line of the setup block (Run report).

Adiabatic (SSR) states

An adiabatic state is an eigenvector 𝐜k of one cassette:

𝐇𝐜k=Ek𝐒𝐜k.

Roots are 0-based and sorted by energy; they are labeled S0, S1, … for 2S zero and T1, Q1, … (counted from 1) for positive 2S (§8). Physical roots exclude the overlap null modes and ghost roots more than 100 Eh above the lowest root (Ghost states and a collapsed SI spectrum); SSR ENERGIES K=e holds every physical root of cassette e (SSR state energies).

Chirgwin–Coulson weights

The weight of configuration j in a state is

ωj=cj(𝐒𝐜)j,∑jωj=1.

In the non-orthogonal configuration set an individual ωj can be negative. Adiabatic states prints them in % (column w_j).

Unrelaxed and relaxed densities

The unrelaxed density of states I, J is the one-particle (transition) density of the model wavefunctions at the fixed orbitals and FONs:

⟨I|Eˆpq|J⟩.

Every SI_REKS_ANALYSIS property uses it (§15). The relaxed density Pr of a state adds the orbital- and FON-response terms of the coupled-perturbed REKS (CP-REKS, Z-vector) solve (Filatov, Liu, Martínez, J. Chem. Phys. 2017, 147, 034113), so that its dipole is the energy derivative

μ=−dEIdF.

It is formed for the SI_REKS_PROPERTY_FOR states. Pr is not N-representable: it can have negative natural occupations, and bond indices from it are stored but not printed (Bond indices of the relaxed density).
